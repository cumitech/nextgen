One note asks to replace the headline with “Turn your business ideas into real opportunities”. The other note says the current headline is good and offers the new line only as a suggestion. The same headline is also used on the sign-in page. We recommend keeping it unless you want one new campaign line on both pages.
- [ ] **Hero buttons.** “Explore courses” is the main button. The second button is “Learn more”, which moves down the home page. “Start learning” is the closing button for guests, and it goes to sign-in, because a guest cannot open a course from the hero. One button called “Explore courses and start learning” would have to point at two different places.
- [ ] **Free and paid courses.** A course with a fee shows its configured amount and currency. On checkout, a USD fee also shows the exact CFA charge when an XAF-only payment gateway is enabled. A course with no fee shows no price and no “Free” label, so a free course and a course with a missing price look the same. A price typed in by hand can also appear as a number with no currency. The remaining gaps are a clear “Free” label and a currency on every hand-entered price.
- [ ] **Enrolment numbers.** Each course card shows how many people have the student role in that course. A new course that says “0 students” is reporting the real number, including any test accounts. We should not raise that number for appearance. We can hide the count until at least one learner is enrolled.
- [x] **Course totals.** The catalogue, category tiles, home-page figures, and About copy use published-course data rather than the framework’s proposed catalogue. Keep draft course titles and counts out of public totals until those courses are approved and published; review any saved custom text that might override the theme defaults.
- [ ] **Institutional packages, sponsored cohorts, and custom programmes.** The home page invites organisations to discuss training needs but makes clear that scope, delivery, and fees must be confirmed. Every course page has “Request a quote”. There is no separate page for an institutional package or sponsored cohort. Group enrolment is set up per course inside Moodle. Add a dedicated page only after the offer, eligibility, delivery, and enquiry process are defined.
- [ ] **Instructors on the home page.** Teacher records are available, and the home page does not show a “Meet instructors” block. Visitors meet the teacher on the course they are considering, which is where that information is used. A home-page row is useful once several teachers are assigned to published courses.

### Recommended website components

1. **Programme and delivery overview:** a concise block for online courses, in-person seminars/workshops, and blended programmes, with the Cameroon-first and future regional focus. The landing copy now covers this at a high level.
2. **Course learning outcomes and practical outputs:** show audience, level, language, prerequisites, hours, format, assessment, and project/output on each course page once approved.
3. **Learner pathway:** explain discovery, enrolment, assessment, practical work, completion, and any verified next-step support without promising services that are not yet operating.
4. **Events calendar:** the new Events page supports published seminars and workshops, with title/description/location search and filters for type, format, and upcoming/all/past dates. Staff can create and edit events from the public site. Event accessibility and intended take-home outputs can be added as fields if those details become a regular requirement.
5. **Verified impact and learner stories:** after the pilot, publish measured competencies and business starts/improvements with methodology, follow-up period, and informed consent.
6. **Regulatory and certificate information:** once verified, state the applicable authorisation, approved specialties, assessment basis, and exact certificate type. Avoid any implication that attendance alone is a recognised qualification.
7. **Institutional training enquiry:** the existing landing-page block is a suitable starting point. Add a dedicated institutional page only after the offer, cohort process, sponsorship terms, and service capacity are defined.
